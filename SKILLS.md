## Agent Skills Overview

* `run-all-validations`: `python3 validators/run_all.py`
* `enrich-draft-dataset`: `python3 tools/enrich_dataset.py --input <file> --output <out> --book "<Title>"`
* `format-json`: `python3 scripts/format_json.py`
* `generate-checksums`: `python3 scripts/generate_checksums.py`
* `renumber-verses`: `python3 scripts/renumber_verses.py <file.json>`
* `download-gretil-txt`: `bash scripts/download_gretil.sh [target_dir] [url]`
* `parse-gretil-headers`: `python3 tools/parse_gretil_headers.py [--dir temp] [--search "Query"]`
* `build-art-datasets`: `python3 tools/build_art_datasets.py [--batch 1-10] [--limit 50] [--workers 4] [--force]`
