# ATTRIBUTION & PROVENANCE STANDARDS

**Organization**: CRINE ([www.crine.in](https://www.crine.in))

---

## Provenance Requirements

Every scripture dataset added to a repository derived from this template must maintain clear, verifiable source provenance in `ATTRIBUTION.md` and `sources.md`:

1. **Source Transparency**: Direct URL to digital source archive or print publication details.
2. **Translator Credit**: Full name, year, and attribution notice for translators and commentators.
3. **Digitization Notes**: Notes on OCR, character encoding, or verse numbering adjustments.

---

# Attribution & Provenance: Example Book
