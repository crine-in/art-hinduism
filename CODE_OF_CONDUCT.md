# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the **All Religious Texts (ART)** community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

We commit to fostering open, respectful, academic, objective, and culturally sensitive dialogue across all religious traditions and scholarly communities.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or disrespectful behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at `conduct@crine.in`.
